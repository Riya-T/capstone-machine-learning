# Stock Portfolio independent replication

The prior study found a direction for Stock Portfolio that survived
all possible outcomes for one stopped trajectory. Its completeness
rule barred confirmation. Preserve that study and every original score.
This new experiment checks the same direction on fresh model draws.

## Fixed inputs

Reuse the exact saved starts sample 00 and sample 03, the two
original change sequences, supplied tests and the same preserved
behavior supplement. Sample 00 stores balances and sample 03 derives
them from the ledger. Sample 03 came from a design-constrained teacher
prompt. Recheck both parents on the complete frozen initial suites and
a separate state oracle. The earlier discovery selects the family and
direction, so this is a same-family replication.

The GPU editor is Qwen3-8B Q8_0 through the verified WSL llama.cpp build.
Keep the prior prompt, sampling, context, token, turn, time and public
feedback settings. No hosted generation, training or new weights.

## Submission and scoring

Keep explicit submission and the existing most recent public-passing
fallback. When an editor reaches a budget limit without either and
its current workspace differs from the start, collect that draft only
if the unchanged parser has applied it and every expected file parses
as Python. Keep its budget-stop reason and report it as an automatic
budget draft. A syntactically complete draft can fail behavior tests.
There is no private feedback or private selection of drafts. Ambiguous
or truncated replies remain ineligible. Absence of a draft, timeout,
backend or evaluator error remains distinct from a code failure.

The evaluator checks the entire saved draft under the original scoring
suites. A privately correct budget draft can reach the next checkpoint
under this new policy. Report a sensitivity calculation that considers
every possible successful prefix for affected trajectories. A difference
that depends on treating a budget draft as a submission remains
operationally interesting and ineligible for the robust preference gate.

Before inference, use fixture controls for explicit submissions,
public-passing fallback, valid budget drafts, malformed final drafts,
unchanged sources, truncated replies, and backend errors. Validate
supplied references and the independently certified initial oracle
before first use. The saved-only diagnostic on the historical stopped
draft is kept separate.

## Schedule and decision

Three matched blocks per sequence, two parents, two successive changes
per trajectory. Seeds are new and matched by block, sequence and
checkpoint. Counterbalance parent order. Stop a trajectory at the first
failed checkpoint. The fixed maximum is 24 reached checkpoints and
two hours from the first actual attempt. Resume keeps deadlines and
receipts. Never retry a started request without a complete receipt.

A preliminary pair requires every trajectory to have a behavioral
outcome, a strict lead for sample 00 on both sequences, at least three
additional successful checkpoints overall, and a lead in at least two
of three matched blocks. The same numerical gate must hold even under
every possible resolution of trajectories whose prefixes depend on
budget drafts. Require saved artifact and trace reproduction, and
inspect failures for starting bugs and specification/scoring confounds
before any preference claim. A reversal is exploratory. Ties, unscorable
cases and confounds stay unlabeled.

A single eligible pair would support testing collection on a separately
selected family. It would not establish generalization or justify a
training run. No training labels are set automatically.
